Frequently Asked Questions

Considering outsourcing?

What businesses do you work with?

Excedere is primarily designed for small and growing businesses looking for a practical way to access global talent or reduce the cost of expanding their team.

Which countries can I outsource to?

The appropriate location depends on the role, working hours, required experience, budget and other business requirements. We discuss those factors before recommending an approach.

How much can outsourcing save?

Savings vary considerably depending on the role, location, experience level and employment model. Rather than quoting an unrealistic headline percentage, we assess the requirements and help you compare the likely cost against your current or planned local hire.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to outsource?

No. That's one of the reasons to speak with us. If you know where the pressure points are in your business, we can explore which functions may be suitable for outsourcing.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. The initial conversation is simply an opportunity to understand your requirements and determine whether outsourcing is worth exploring.
